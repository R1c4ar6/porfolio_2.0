## Design Context

### Users
Tech recruiters evaluating frontend skill and attention to detail. They scan quickly for technical competence, design sensibility, and professionalism.

### Brand Personality
Professional, Clean, Modern — competent and polished without being flashy. Builds trust through precision and restraint.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Tone**: Clean, refined, subtly bold — professional enough for enterprise, distinctive enough to be remembered
- **Theme**: Both light and dark mode, system-preference driven
- **Colors**: Muted slate-based palette with a sharp blue accent for interactive elements
- **Typography**: Geist (sans) for body/headlines, Geist Mono for technical labels — clean and modern
- **Anti-references**: Avoid glassmorphism, gradient text, neon-on-dark, or any "AI-generated" aesthetic

### Design Principles
1. **Precision over decoration** — every element earns its place; spacing, alignment, and typography do the heavy lifting
2. **Subtle responsiveness** — interactions feel immediate and intentional (ease-out transitions, transform/opacity only)
3. **Consistent restraint** — one accent color, two font families, deliberate whitespace
4. **Accessible by default** — focus indicators, contrast, reduced motion, keyboard navigation
5. **Ship with zero rough edges** — no typos, no hardcoded colors, no console logs, no layout shift
